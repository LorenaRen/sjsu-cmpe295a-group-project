2) Accomplished During This Meeting
- She walked us through her prior paper in detail:
  - Title: "Standards-Aligned Ethical Gating and Decision Telemetry for LLM-Assisted Mobile Robot Navigation: A Simulation Study"
  - Published in 2026 at IEEE BigDataService, by Preet Kamalnayan Pandit, Aryan Singh, Hiten Bharatkumar Galiya, and Wu. Funded by NSF grant RINGS-2148353.
  - How it works: the LLM is only an advisor and never gets direct control. It suggests GO/SLOW/STOP, and then a deterministic safety/privacy gate and arbiter check that suggestion and can override it. Everything gets logged.
  - It's grounded in IEEE 7001, BS 8611, and the NIST AI RMF.
  - They tested it in ROS 2 Jazzy + Gazebo Harmonic with a single differential-drive robot, using Phi-3.5-mini as the LLM.
  - Zero safety violations across 2,400 trials, which is pretty impressive. The one limitation they call out themselves is that they only ever tested one small, local LLM.
